# FlowDepot Client Live Simulation Guide

Use the seeded demo accounts from the login screen. For the broadest walkthrough, sign in as Owner or Warehouse Manager and open **Live Simulation**.

Recommended client sequence:
1. Open **Inventory Control** to show on-hand/reserved/available stock, unit cost/price, handling flags and the stock ledger.
2. Open **Delivery Planning** to show customer ad-hoc requests, the review queue, unplanned deliveries and driver/vehicle schedules.
3. Start **Full warehouse-to-POD workflow** in Live Simulation. The feed demonstrates goods request, reservation, delivery creation, dispatch plan, primary/standby driver, fragile-goods instructions, driver acknowledgement, GPS arrival and POD distribution to registered receiving/manager contacts.
4. Open **Deliveries & Dispatch** to show route/stop execution and standby coverage.
5. Open **POD Contacts** to show one-or-more receiving contacts and managers per destination.

All simulation records are tagged and resettable. This edition must not be pointed at live operational data.
